Our Story

Built on Trust,
Backed by Experience

Duramax Body Shop was founded in 1989 by Pierre Lefever, whose passion for excellence and dedication to craftsmanship built the foundation of what has become one of Maryland's most trusted auto body shops. Pierre's commitment to doing things right — every time — set the standard that defines everything we do.

Following Pierre's passing in 2014, his wife Florencia Gradillas stepped in to lead the business. Under her leadership, Duramax has grown significantly in both reach and reputation, while honoring the values Pierre built. Today, a 4.9-star rating across 170+ reviews reflects the trust our community has placed in us for over three decades.
