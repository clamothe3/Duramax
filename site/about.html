Our Story

Built on Trust,
Backed by Experience

Duramax Body Shop was founded in 1989 with a simple mission: deliver honest, high-quality auto body repair to the Kensington community. What started as a small local shop has grown into one of Maryland's most recognized collision and restoration centers.

Over the decades, we've invested in state-of-the-art equipment and ongoing training to keep our technicians at the cutting edge — while never losing the personal, family-owned touch that our customers depend on.
